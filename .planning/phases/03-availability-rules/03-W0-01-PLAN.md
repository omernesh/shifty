**Why §8.3 needs amending now (not later):**

The current §8.3 "Enforcement" paragraph (PRD line 778) reads: *"The four-layer defense is: (1) session has a tenant_id; (2) query has a tenant_id filter; (3) page has an auth block; (4) request has a server-side role check. Missing any one of these is a release-blocking bug."*

A "5-layer" amendment paragraph was already appended (PRD lines 780–795) during the 2026-05-17 post-pivot revision. The amendment text is correct and load-bearing. This plan's job is to verify the amendment is complete, consistent, and properly cross-linked — NOT to rewrite it from scratch. The amendment may already be in its final shape; verify the final pass for editorial polish only.

**Empirical evidence the amendment cites** (already in BUDIBASE-CONVENTIONS.md §2, do not duplicate):
1. Budibase Postgres integration cannot wrap queries in transactions — `SET LOCAL` is silently discarded.
2. Multi-statement query bodies crash the JS integration.
3. `{{ Current User.tenantId }}` bindings are parameterized (extended protocol), not textual; `SET LOCAL` rejects bound params.
4. Budibase connects as the `shifts` Postgres superuser; per `pg_authid.rolsuper` semantics, superusers bypass RLS unconditionally.

**What MUST be present in the final §8.3 amendment paragraph:**
- (a) Layer 5 inactive for Budibase clients due to superuser bypass (with the four concrete blockers above, summarized).
- (b) Layer 5 policies preserved in schema for future direct-DB consumers — name the FastAPI solver (Phase 4) explicitly.
- (c) Layer 2 (`WHERE tenant_id = '{{ Current User.tenantId }}'::uuid`) is the new top defense for Budibase-mediated queries.
- (d) The CI gate `tools/check-bb-queries.mjs` (built in W0-04) is the enforcement mechanism.
- (e) "Missing any layer is release-blocking" rule applies to the effective layer map; Layer 5 inactivity is the explicit framework constraint, not a missing layer.
- (f) Cross-reference to `docs/BUDIBASE-CONVENTIONS.md` §2 for the empirical evidence.
